Need help using SSC With Jagrat?

Tap any feature below to see a simple step-by-step guide in English and Hindi.

📝Exam SimulatorHow to start a 25/50/100 question mock⌄
English

Open Tests → Exam Simulator. Choose the exam type, subject/topic or full mock, then select 25, 50 or 100 questions. Start the test, use the question palette to move between questions, mark questions for review, clear an answer when needed, and submit before or when the timer ends. Your result shows your score and performance.

हिंदी

Tests → Exam Simulator खोलें। Exam type, subject/topic या full mock चुनें और 25, 50 या 100 questions चुनें। Test शुरू करें। Question palette से questions बदलें, जरूरत हो तो Mark for Review करें, answer clear करें और timer खत्म होने से पहले या timer पर test submit करें। इसके बाद score और performance दिखाई जाएगी।

🤖AI Study AssistantAsk GK, GS, Maths, Reasoning and English questions⌄
English

Open AI from the bottom navigation or hamburger menu. Type your question in the chat and send it. You can ask for explanations, concepts, examples, practice help, or doubts from GK/GS, Maths, Reasoning and English.

हिंदी

Bottom navigation या hamburger menu से AI खोलें। Chat में अपना सवाल लिखकर भेजें। GK/GS, Maths, Reasoning और English के concepts, explanations, examples, practice या doubts पूछ सकते हैं।

🧠AI Smart MockGenerate an AI practice mock⌄
English

Inside Exam Simulator, choose AI Smart Mock. Select the exam, subject, chapter or custom focus and choose 25/50/100 questions. Start the generated mock. These are AI Practice questions, not verified official PYQs.

हिंदी

Exam Simulator में AI Smart Mock चुनें। Exam, subject, chapter या custom focus और 25/50/100 questions चुनें। Generated mock शुरू करें। ये AI Practice questions हैं, verified official PYQs नहीं।

📚GK Batches & ClassesOpen Parmar GK 4.0 or 5.0 and find classes⌄
English

From My Batch, choose PARMAR GK BATCH 4.0 or 5.0. Open a subject using its arrow, then choose the required class. Videos and class PDFs are organised separately for each batch.

हिंदी

My Batch में PARMAR GK BATCH 4.0 या 5.0 चुनें। Subject के arrow पर tap करें और फिर अपनी class चुनें। Videos और class PDFs दोनों batches के लिए अलग-अलग organised हैं।

🗺️Mind MapsOpen English or Hindi PDF notes⌄
English

Open a subject first. At the top of the subject page you will find Mind Maps. Choose [Subject] (English) or [Subject] (Hindi) to open the PDF.

हिंदी

पहले कोई subject खोलें। Subject page के ऊपर Mind Maps section मिलेगा। PDF खोलने के लिए [Subject] (English) या [Subject] (Hindi) पर tap करें।

🏛️History: Ancient / Medieval / ModernChoose the History part you want⌄
English

Open History from a batch. Mind Maps appear first. Below them, choose Ancient, Medieval or Modern History. Each section opens its own classes and videos.

हिंदी

Batch से History खोलें। सबसे पहले Mind Maps मिलेंगे। उसके नीचे Ancient, Medieval या Modern History चुनें। हर section की अपनी classes और videos हैं।
